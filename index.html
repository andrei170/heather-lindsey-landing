FAQ

Common questions, before applying.

How is this different from the bundle?

The bundle ($2,996) is the methodology + the strategy stack delivered as four self-paced courses. The Inner Circle is the 12-month cohort room above it. Same methodology. Same teacher. Different format. Application-only, capped cohort, monthly group calls with Heather, quarterly retreats, and a peer community running the same playbook with you.

Do I need to have completed the bundle first?

Not strictly required, but strongly preferred. The Inner Circle assumes you know the 5 simple steps and have run trades on the Think or Swim platform. If you are earlier than that, start with Level 1 first and then apply to the Inner Circle once you have walked the ladder. The application form will route you appropriately.

What is the time commitment?

3-4 hours per week. One monthly group call with Heather (90 minutes, recorded). Daily check-in optional in the cohort community. One quarterly in-person retreat (2-3 days, 4 per year, optional but strongly encouraged).

What's the price?

Stage-specific, confirmed at intake. The Inner Circle is application-only; pricing is shared on the intake call once we have confirmed cohort fit. Both annual and split-pay options are available.

How big is the cohort?

Small. Capped per round to keep the monthly group calls real and the peer accountability tight. Two cohorts per year, founding cohort opens [date].

Where are the retreats?

The first founding-cohort retreat is at a US conference venue (location confirmed at intake). Future retreats rotate. All retreats are designed around school-holidays and workweek-friendly calendars - because working adults need that flexibility.

Refund policy?

Yes. First 30 days, full refund if the room is not the right fit, no questions. The cohort is designed for committed traders, not casual sign-ups - if you are unsure, the intake call is the place to ask.

What if I cannot make the live monthly call?

Recordings posted within 48 hours. Cohort community is asynchronous, so the daily check-in works around your schedule. The retreats are quarterly, scheduled with 90-day notice, designed for working-adult calendars.

Why do I have to apply?

The cohort is small and the room only works if everyone in it is at a similar stage with the methodology. Application is a 4-minute form; we get back to you within 48 hours. If the cohort is full or the timing is not right, we route you to the waitlist for the next round or to the right course tier in the meantime.
